## Best-Fit Customer (Primary Segment)

**Profile:** Agency founder/owner, $10k–$100k/month revenue, 2–15 team members (employees + contractors), actively closing clients monthly (inbound/outbound), experiencing admin overload/delivery friction/backend chaos, client onboarding involves manual coordination, internal tasks require constant founder oversight, tools exist but don't fully connect, craves scalable processes but lacks time/expertise to build them.

**Qualification Criteria (all must be true):**
- [ ] 2–15 team members involved in delivery or operations
- [ ] Already clearing $10k–$100k+/month
- [ ] Actively closing clients every month (inbound/outbound)
- [ ] Experiencing admin overload, delivery friction, or backend chaos
- [ ] Client onboarding still involves manual coordination, emails, or Slack chaos
- [ ] Internal tasks still require constant founder oversight
- [ ] Tools like CRM, Notion, Slack, or project systems exist but don't fully connect
- [ ] Craves scalable processes but lacks time/expertise to build them
- [ ] Founder still involved in tasks they shouldn't be touching

**Disqualification Criteria (any = not a fit):**
- [ ] Solo freelancer without a team or delivery structure
- [ ] Not generating $10k+/month yet
- [ ] Still figuring out how to get clients
- [ ] Enjoys drowning in spreadsheets, manual follow-ups, chaotic workflows
- [ ] Looking for templates instead of real infrastructure
- [ ] Expects a quick tool setup instead of serious backend rebuild
- [ ] Unwilling to give backend access for real optimization
- [ ] Thinks "automation" means slapping a chatbot on website
- [ ] Would rather waste 20+ hours/week on busywork than invest in growth
