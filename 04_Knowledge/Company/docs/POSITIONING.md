Agency founder/owner, $10k–$100k/month revenue, 2–15 team members, actively closing clients monthly, experiencing admin overload/delivery friction/backend chaos, client onboarding involves manual coordination, internal tasks require constant founder oversight, tools exist but don't fully connect, craves scalable processes but lacks time/expertise to build them.
